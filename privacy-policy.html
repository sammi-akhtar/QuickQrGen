Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 21, 2026

At QuickQRGen ("we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our free QR code generator service. Please read this privacy policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Client-Side Only Service

QuickQRGen operates as a completely client-side service. This means:

  • We do NOT collect any personal information
  • We do NOT store any QR codes or content you generate
  • All QR code generation happens in your browser
  • No data is transmitted to our servers

1.2 Information You Provide

When you use our service, you may enter information to generate QR codes such as:

  • Website URLs
  • WiFi credentials (SSID, password)
  • Contact information (for vCard QR codes)
  • Text content
  • Other data you choose to encode in QR codes

This information is processed solely in your browser and is never transmitted to or stored by us.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, we may collect certain information automatically when you visit our site:

  • Log Data: Our hosting provider may automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages, and dates/times of visits. This is standard practice for all websites.
  • Cookies: We may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, but these do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • Analytics: We may use third-party analytics services to understand how visitors use our site, but these are configured to respect privacy and do not track individuals.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we operate as a client-side service:

  • We do NOT use any personal information you enter for QR generation (as we don't collect it)
  • Any automatically collected data is used solely for:
    • Improving our website performance
    • Understanding general usage patterns
    • Enhancing user experience
    • Maintaining website security

3. Advertising

QuickQRGen is supported by advertising. We use third-party advertising services including:

  • Google AdSense: Our primary advertising partner
  • Start.io: Our fallback advertising partner

3.1 Ad Privacy Policies

These advertising services may use cookies and similar technologies to serve relevant ads. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies:

  • Google AdSense Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Start.io Privacy Policy: https://www.start.io/privacy

3.2 Ad Blocking

We respect your choice to use ad blockers. If an ad blocker is detected, we may show a polite message asking you to consider disabling it to support our free service. We will never:

  • Block content based on ad blocker usage
  • Force you to disable your ad blocker
  • Track or penalize users who use ad blockers

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect any automatically collected data:

  • Secure hosting environment
  • Regular security updates
  • Limited data retention
  • No storage of sensitive personal information

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any website you visit.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Since we do not collect or store personal information:

  • All data processing happens locally in your browser

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

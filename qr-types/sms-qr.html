SMS QR Codes: The Complete Guide

SMS QR codes make it incredibly easy to send text messages to specific phone numbers. When scanned, these QR codes automatically open the user's SMS/messaging app with the recipient number and optional message pre-filled.

Why Use SMS QR Codes?

  • Instant Messaging: Users can send SMS messages with just one scan
  • Pre-filled Messages: Include default text for common inquiries
  • Customer Support: Easy access to support SMS lines
  • Lead Generation: Collect customer contact information
  • Marketing Campaigns: SMS-based promotional campaigns
  • Emergency Communications: Quick access to emergency SMS numbers

How SMS QR Codes Work

SMS QR codes use the "sms:" protocol, which is a standard way for mobile devices to initiate text messaging. When scanned:

  1. The QR code contains an sms: link (e.g., sms:+15551234567?body=Hello)
  2. Scanning opens the user's default messaging app
  3. The recipient number is automatically entered
  4. Optional message text is pre-filled in the compose area

Best Practices for SMS QR Codes

1. Include Country Codes

Always use international format (+country code) for reliable delivery

2. Keep Messages Concise

SMS messages are limited to 160 characters, so keep pre-filled text brief

3. Test on Multiple Devices

Different messaging apps may handle SMS links differently

4. Clear Purpose

Include context like "Scan to text us" or "Send us a message"

5. Professional Numbers

Use business SMS numbers rather than personal mobile numbers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SMS QR Code?

An SMS QR Code contains an sms: link that, when scanned, opens the user's messaging app with a pre-filled phone number and optional message text.

Can I include a message in SMS QR codes?

Yes! You can pre-fill message text using the format: sms:+15551234567?body=Your%20message%20here

Do SMS QR codes work internationally?

Yes, when you include country codes (+1, +44, +91, etc.), SMS QR codes work internationally and will use local SMS rates.

What's the SMS character limit?

Standard SMS messages are limited to 160 characters. Longer messages will be split into multiple SMS parts.
